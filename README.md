# My Manhwa Shelf!

*A little corner of the internet for my manhwa collection.*

My Manhwa Shelf! is a website I built because I wanted a place to keep track of all the manhwas I read, the ones I'm currently reading, the ones I stopped reading, and the ones I want to read someday. I wanted it to feel more personal than a regular reading tracker. Like somewhere I could organise my collection, rate my favourites, leave side notes, and have everything in one place. I was mostly inspired by Letterboxd.

**[Take a look at My Manhwa Shelf!](https://my-manhwa-shelf.vercel.app/)**

## What You Can Do 

**Have your own shelf**: Create an account and access your personal collection.
**Keep track of your collection** — Add your manhwa and keep everything organized in one place.
**Add your own covers**: Give each entry a cover image.
**Rate your reads**: Rate manhwas with a heart-based rating system.
**Track your progress**: Sort titles into Reading, Want to Read, Dropped, or Completed.
**Add tags and genres**: Organise your collection in a way that works for you.
**Leave side notes**: Save your thoughts about a story you want to remember.
**See your stats**: Check your collection size and average rating.
**Find your titles**: Browse and manage your library more easily.

And, of course, I wanted the whole thing to have a cozy bookshelf-inspired look with my favourite colours!

## The tools I used to bring this project to life:

* **Next.js**: For building the web app.
* **TypeScript**: For writing more reliable code.
* **Firebase Authentication**: For user sign-up and login.
* **Cloud Firestore**: For storing each user's manhwa collection.
* **CSS Modules**: For styling the different parts of the app.
* **Vercel**: For deployment and hosting.

## Why I Made This

I wanted a dedicated place to keep up with the manhwas I was reading, save the ones I wanted to check out later, and keep track of the stories I'd finished. I also wanted it to feel nice to use. A plain list of titles didn't really vibe with me. Especially when apps like Letterboxd, Goodreads, MyAnimeList, etc. exists to keep track of other media I consume. So, I decided to build something that felt more like a personal bookshelf (And like, I realised I am a CS student. Why can't I just make one??). Along the way, I got to work with Next.js, TypeScript, Firebase Authentication, and Firestore, and learned more about putting together and deploying a full-stack web app. It started with a simple idea, and I had fun turning it into something I could actually use.

There are still plenty of things I'd like for it to have. But it was my first time doing something like this. Plus there are already the main features that i wanted. So, I decided to not make it any more complicated. For now, I'm happy to have a little space of my own for all things manhwas.
